Introduction

Motivation for MIST/MISFIT, the three BraTS 2026 tasks (metastases, pediatric tumors, BraTS-GoAT), and the two questions this study asks (system-level deployment vs. isolated pretraining effect).

Methods

Data (Table 1: task, cohort, training/held-out counts, regions), preprocessing, MISFIT self-supervised MAE pretraining, the three network arms (pocket nnUNet baseline, MISFIT-pretrained SwinUNETR-V2, from-scratch SwinUNETR-V2), and the evaluation protocol (cross-validation vs. held-out, BraTS-style rank, Wilcoxon test).

Results

Table 2 (average rank + p-values), Table 3 (per-region metrics), Figure 1 (validation loss curves), Figure 2 (qualitative comparison).

Discussion & Conclusion

Held-out validation does not favor the pooled MISFIT pipeline over the pocket nnUNet baseline; matched-control comparison shows MAE pretraining helps when architecture/patch size/budget are fixed; final challenge submission uses the compliant baseline.
